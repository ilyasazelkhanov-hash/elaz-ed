## Publish safely

These are story rows, not blog news or Telegram broadcasts. Import only through an authorised admin path after title/body duplicate checks. Do not award XP for reading an advert: xp=0. Do not overwrite existing material. Read back rows after insertion.

Offers use category `Выгода`, `Акции` or `Скидки`. They require checked_on, future review_due and primary sources. The frontend hides them on review_due until an editor verifies and renews them. ## Article formats, not random minutes

A brief update may need 80-180 words. A practical example can take 250-450. A comparison or experiment can take 600-1000 only when it adds evidence, steps and caveats. These are editorial options, not mandatory quotas. Never stretch a short announcement or invent experience to create variety.

Reading estimate uses visible words at 180/min, rounded up, not time to complete the task. The admin auto-estimate and publication script share it. The open article recomputes from its body. Feed cards with no body still use stored minutes; existing database rows need a guarded metadata-only correction after inspection. This commit does not change the production database or rewrite old articles.
